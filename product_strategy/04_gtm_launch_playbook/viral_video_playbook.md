## 1. Short-Form Production Guidelines

To achieve top-quartile performance (>80% completion rate, >15% share rate) on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts algorithms, adhere to these technical standards:

1. **Aspect Ratio & Resolution**: Vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920 pixels, minimum 60 fps).
2. **Safe-Zone Layout**: Keep all critical text overlays, captions, and visual punchlines within the vertical safe zone (avoiding bottom 350px covered by captions/sound titles and right-side 150px covered by engagement buttons).
3. **Pacing & Edit Cadence**:
   - The hook must execute within the first **2.8 seconds**.
   - Camera cut or visual focal shift every **1.5 to 2.2 seconds** to prevent visual habituation.
4. **Dynamic Captions**: High-contrast, word-by-word animated subtitles (yellow/white bold typography on dark shadow) placed in the upper-middle third of the frame.
5. **Sound Design Priority**: Dialogue must be crisp and punchy, overlaid on a trending or high-retention audio bed. Diegetic sound effects (mechanical keyboard clicks, notification chimes, vine booms, record scratches) must synchronize frame-accurately with visual transitions.

To scale short-form video volume rapidly post-launch, recruit creators across 3 primary niches:

1. **Tech & Software Engineering Humor** (Audience: Software engineers, CS students, tech workers).
2. **ADHD & Neurodivergent Life Hacks** (Audience: Students, knowledge workers struggling with executive function).
3. **Aesthetic Desk Setups & Productivity** (Audience: Designers, remote workers, Notion/minimalist setup enthusiasts).

- **Free Core Application**: $0 forever to ensure maximum top-of-funnel install velocity.
