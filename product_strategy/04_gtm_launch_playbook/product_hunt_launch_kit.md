## 5. Comprehensive Launch FAQ

### Q1: Does this app record my keystrokes, passwords, or personal documents?
**Answer**: Absolutely not. Our `KeyboardTracker` (`bihari/spy.py`) is mathematically incapable of keylogging. It utilizes `pynput` listeners solely to append `time.monotonic()` float values into rolling double-ended queues (`collections.deque(maxlen=100)`). It evaluates timestamp deltas to calculate characters per minute (velocity) and backspace ratios (friction). It does not inspect, parse, or persist character codes or string values.

Furthermore, our `PrivacyFilter` (`bihari/privacy.py`) automatically intercepts window titles before processing. Any window belonging to password managers (`1Password`, `Bitwarden`, `KeePassXC`) or containing sensitive strings (`bank`, `login`, `password`, `checkout`, `credit card`) is immediately overwritten with `HIDDEN_PRIVACY_CONTEXT` and excluded from OCR.

### Q2: Does the screen OCR send screenshots of my desktop to a cloud server?
**Answer**: No. Zero network packets are ever transmitted. The application uses Windows 11's native `Windows.Media.Ocr` API via `winocr` and Win32 GDI screen capture. The capture is strictly constrained to the bounding box of the active foreground window, processed entirely within volatile system RAM, and immediately garbage-collected (<30ms execution). No images are ever saved to disk or uploaded to any remote server. You can run the entire application with Wi-Fi disabled in Airplane Mode.

### Q3: Won't running background OCR and AI drain my laptop battery and slow down games?
**Answer**: No. The system was designed from day one for near-zero resource consumption:
1. **Event-Gated**: OCR does not poll continuously. It only evaluates upon significant window switches or detected scrolling trance states.
2. **Sub-30ms Execution**: Native WinRT OCR executes in <30ms on modern CPUs.
3. **Deterministic Caching**: Classification results are cached by window title hash.
4. **Laya 421M Decision Model**: Our local classification model is non-autoregressive and optimized for CPU execution (<15ms latency). During active deep work and idle periods, the application consumes **0.0% CPU** and ~45MB of RAM.

### Q4: Is this just a novelty joke app, or is there real psychological utility?
**Answer**: It is grounded in clinical behavioral psychology—specifically **Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)** and the principle of **Cognitive Defusion**. Traditional website blockers rely on punitive restriction, which triggers psychological reactance (rebellion against external control). 

Becoming a Bihari uses wordless visual humor as a pattern interrupt. Subcortical image comprehension occurs in under 150ms—far faster than verbal reasoning. By confronting the user with a gentle, humorous mirror rather than a scolding message, it creates a split-second pause of metacognitive awareness, allowing executive function to resume control voluntarily.

You can hot-swap between both tracks at any time with a single click from the system tray menu.

### Q6: Can I create and share custom meme packs with my friends or team?
**Answer**: Yes! Extensibility is central to the product. Meme packs follow the `.lucidpack` specification. A pack is simply a folder containing a `pack.json` manifest, an optional `reflections.toml` custom text file, and folders organized by vibe (`in-the-zone/`, `fighting-the-code/`, `lost-in-the-scroll/`, etc.). You can drop your own PNGs, JPEGs, and GIFs into these folders, or package them into a `.zip` archive to share.
